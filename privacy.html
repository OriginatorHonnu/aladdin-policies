Aladdin - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Aladdin, a Discord bot, handles information when users interact with it.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled by server administrators, Aladdin may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, message content needed to operate commands and features, and configuration settings.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide bot commands, moderation, server configuration, welcome messages, tickets, activity features, and other enabled functionality.

4. Storage and Retention

Some configuration or feature data may be stored to keep Aladdin working. The actual retention period depends on the bot's hosting and storage configuration.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord and the hosting or storage providers required to operate the bot.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

Server administrators can disable or remove bot features. Users can contact the developer with privacy-related questions or requests.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Aladdin developer through the official support channel, if available.

9. Updates

This policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change.
